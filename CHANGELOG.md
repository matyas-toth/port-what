## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-07

### Added

- Cross-platform TCP and UDP port-owner discovery.
- Compact process details and parent ancestry.
- Graceful process-tree termination with forced fallback and port verification.
- Support for `npx`, global installation, color control, and standard help/version flags.
- Unit and end-to-end tests across Linux, macOS, and Windows CI runners.
